# Project UI preferences

- Photographer portfolio editing follows approved option 1: form beside a compact preview, tabs for introduction/working approach/concept photos, draft/preview/publish actions. Use the same PhotographerProfile public snapshot and PhotographerPortfolio renderer as customer pages and booking profiles; saving a draft never changes public content or concept assignments. Only the owner edits their draft; admin shares revision checks. Public portfolios are concept-only. Approved on 2026-11-13.

- The personal photographer Dashboard uses the approved statistics layout: six metrics (shoots, upcoming 7 days, editing, awaiting acceptance, completed, overdue), a service-split workload chart and a priority queue. Do not show “Lịch chụp gần nhất” or personal unavailability there. Filters include period, concept/yearbook and the existing season picker. Drilldowns and order Back links preserve dashboard context. Count only assigned work; use actual confirmed shoots, recorded completion events and explicit deadlines rather than inventing dates. Approved on 2026-11-13.

- Do not show the personal “Lịch bận của tôi” section in the photographer's photography-order workspace. Requested on 2026-11-13.

- In the personal photographer workspace, “Đơn chụp ảnh” lists assigned concept and yearbook orders together, with a “Tất cả / Concept / Kỉ yếu” service filter and shared server pagination. Hide the separate “Chụp concept” menu for these accounts; keep existing manager navigation. Preserve the service/status/page context when opening either order type and returning to the list. Requested on 2026-11-13.

- Photographer-only accounts use “Lịch chụp của tôi” as a complete monthly calendar with day details. Include only shoots assigned to the authenticated photographer as lead or support; enforce this in the API. Keep the existing staff navigation and admin/sales management calendar. Month boundaries and today use Vietnam time; load all assignments in the 42-day grid rather than one paginated list page. Approved on 2026-11-13.

- All Back navigation controls (including “Quay lại”, “Trở lại” and links back to a list or order) must have no text underline in default, hover, focus or active states. Preserve keyboard focus outlines. Use the shared `app-back-link` class for new Back links/buttons; existing `customer-back-link`, `concept-back-link` and `concept-time-back` classes follow the same global rule. This is an explicit user preference recorded on 2026-11-11 and applies to future screens and mockups too.
- Photographer portfolios are dedicated profile pages, never profile popups. Prioritize the photographer's identity, personal voice, signature style and working approach; work images are supporting evidence. Each profile stores its own editable content and images. Do not invent celebrity affiliations, awards, experience counts or reviews for seeded studio photographers.
- Concept-booking Back controls go to the immediately preceding logical step, include a left-arrow icon and appear once per screen. Wardrobe information goes back to photographer selection, not package details.
- The yearbook booking entry is labeled “Chụp kỉ yếu”, including desktop/mobile menus and page titles; keep its existing booking routes.
- Customers provide size, height (cm) and weight (kg) for included concept wardrobe; studio prepares the outfits. Do not restore customer-facing catalog item/stock quantity selection in this step.
- Concept wardrobe measurements and shoot brief share one preparation screen after photographer selection. It includes contact details, participant count, measurements and ideas/reference links. Back goes to photographer selection; Continue goes directly to review. Preserve the old `brief` URL as a redirect to the merged screen.
- Concept packages with `maxPeople: 1` show a fixed one-person preparation form without a people dropdown. Packages with a larger maximum keep the participant selector.
- In “Đơn của tôi”, concept orders use the same list/preview/progress layout as the other two services. Customer status filters and badges are limited to “Đã cọc”, “Đang bàn giao”, “Hoàn thành” and “Hủy”.
- All payment screens show a countdown based on the payment's `expiresAt`, update every second, preserve the deadline on reload, and hide expired QR/payment controls. Reuse the shared `PaymentCountdown` for new screens.
- Customer deposits for rentals, concept shoots and yearbook bookings share `DepositCheckout`, opened with `?payment=deposit` on the existing order route. Keep the original customer navbar and omit mockup/review toolbars. Order profiles link to this checkout instead of embedding a second deposit QR. Preserve order snapshot amounts, service-specific confirmation rules and the contextual return link.
- “Đặt lại dịch vụ” on an expired/cancelled deposit restores the saved order's customer inputs into a fresh review draft for the same account, including yearbook students, concept measurements/references and rental selections/dates. Preserve subsequent edits and retry identity across reload. Recheck current availability/prices/terms; require fresh consent and a new submission identity. Keep the old order, payments and expired QR separate from the new booking.
- Customer concept order profiles follow the yearbook order profile layout and shared customer surfaces, controls, price breakdown and progress presentation. Keep the contextual Back link to the originating orders filter/page/selection.
- The main application uses the existing `studio_db` selected by `backend/.env` (`4001`/`5002`); never automatically switch it to a demo database. On 2026-11-12 the user explicitly selected SePay provider Test mode on this main application: `SEPAY_ENABLED=true`, `SEPAY_MODE=sandbox`, `SEPAY_TEST_ON_MAIN_DB=true` in local development. Internal payment simulation stays disabled on `studio_db`. The isolated `4013`/`5013` sandbox remains optional; the active main-app webhook tunnel forwards to `5002`.
- Yearbook order status “Đang thực hiện” appears only within that record's confirmed shoot window (Vietnam time, start inclusive/end exclusive). Before the window show “Đã chốt lịch”, after it show “Đang hậu kỳ” until delivery/completion; cancelled/rejected and delivered/completed states take priority. Lists, filters and profiles share this rule; proposed schedules alone must never activate it.
- New rental orders require an advance of 30% of the full rental fee (daily rate × quantity × inclusive rental days), rounded up to whole VND. The advance counts toward the fee; collect the remaining 70% before handover. Do not require the legacy per-item security deposit configuration or add the advance on top of the rental total. Preserve the amounts and refundable security-deposit terms on existing order snapshots.
- After submitting a new rental, navigate directly to its deposit payment screen (`/account/rentals/:id?payment=deposit`). Customer orders awaiting deposit resume at this screen, show “Chờ thanh toán cọc” and require no manual approval. Reuse the payment countdown and update to confirmation automatically after the deposit is received.
